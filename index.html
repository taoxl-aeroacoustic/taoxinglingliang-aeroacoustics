Flow solver

GPU-accelerated lattice Boltzmann computation with moving rotor walls and an outer acoustic sponge. Both wall conditions use the medium grid and run in sequence on one GPU.

Rotor and acquisition

At 6,666.67 rpm, nine blades give a 1,000 Hz blade-passing frequency. The scheduled statistical window spans 32 turns, or 0.288 seconds at operating speed.

9 blades · 1 kHz BPF

Pressure and radiation

Five duct sections supply circumferential pressure samples. The separate FW-H surface supplies source histories for the external receivers. Duct pressure here includes hydrodynamic fluctuations.
